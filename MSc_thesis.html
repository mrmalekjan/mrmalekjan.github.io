Robust MIMO Secondary Frequency Modeling and Control

M.Sc. Thesis • Amirkabir University of Technology • 2021–2024

University: Amirkabir University of Technology (Tehran Polytechnic)

Degree: M.Sc. in Electrical-Control Engineering

Duration: 2021 – 2024

GPA: 15.84 / 20 (3.44 / 4.0)

Thesis Overview

This thesis presents robust MIMO secondary frequency modeling and control of the Shahid Rajaei combined cycle power plant. The work combines classic control methods with deep learning techniques to address frequency regulation challenges in large-scale power generation systems.

Key Contributions

  • Development of robust MIMO models for secondary frequency control of combined cycle power plants
  • Application of classical control methods alongside deep learning techniques
  • Case study on the Shahid Rajaei combined cycle power plant
  • Performance evaluation under various operating conditions and disturbances

Technical Methods

  • Modeling: MIMO system identification and frequency-domain analysis
  • Control Design: Robust classical controllers (H-infinity, pole placement)
  • Machine Learning: Deep learning models for frequency prediction and adaptive control
  • Tools: MATLAB, Python, TensorFlow, PyTorch
  • Simulation: Power plant dynamic simulation and closed-loop validation

Outcomes

  • Improved frequency regulation robustness under load variations
  • Reduced settling time and overshoot in secondary frequency response
  • Comparison between classic and AI-enhanced control strategies
  • Validated through simulation on real plant data
